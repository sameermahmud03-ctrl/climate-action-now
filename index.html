OUR MISSION

Climate Action Now (CAN) is committed to combating climate change through advocacy, education, and community initiatives. Our goal is to reduce carbon emissions, promote sustainable practices, and drive policy changes to ensure a healthier planet for future generations.

You may already know that our planet is warming up at an alarming rate which if not done something about it, could lead to irreversible consequences in the near future. As part of our efforts, we also strive to address and target directly the factors for this ongoing phenomenon to try to balance the effects of global warming, as well as protect and help those who have already been affected by them.
